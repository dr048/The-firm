A SMALL BUSINESS FINANCE GUIDE
START HERE / 5 MIN READ

Three numbers worth knowing every month

Good financial habits don’t need to be complicated. Start with these three simple figures to build a more useful picture of your business health.

  1. Cash on hand — what’s available right now?
  2. Money coming in — what have you earned and what’s outstanding?
  3. Money going out — what does it take to keep things running?

Reviewing these regularly can help you spot patterns early and make decisions with a little more confidence.
